  identity for every desired active asset. Prior and desired asset IDs/order/active paths must
  match, and all candidate staging paths are unique and disjoint from active paths.

  environment, including rollback identities, remains part of the immutable plan hash.

  installed and active environment revisions, exact installed/active asset maps, exact pinned
  project-instruction references, fixed baseline/candidate probe evidence, readiness, unexplained
  paths, and rollback preservation evidence.
- Install proves desired bytes without activation while the prior worker remains ready. Activate
  is separately event-revision-fenced and requires the worker to remain unavailable. Run/probe and
  observe require the exact desired environment and a ready worker. Rollback proves the exact
  prior revision/assets and passing baseline probes.

  retains the lock. Explained bad bytes/revisions/behavior use the separately journaled rollback.
  Unexplained paths fence progress and can be reported as terminally preserved by exact rollback.

  safety, and skill safety. Candidate evidence must retain or strengthen every baseline claim.
- t3-steward remains scheduling/workflow authority; UpKeeper remains deployment/rollback
  authority; Git owns repository/worktree state; homelab-infra Git plus Ansible remain homelab

  not be proven during rollback.
- Generic UpKeeper revision axes did not inspect per-asset environment revisions, fixed behavioral
  probes, project-instruction pins, worker readiness, or unexplained environment drift.
- `component=agent-environment` could omit `AgentEnvironmentSpec` and bypass its gates.
- Path normalization and staging/active separation were not enforced across the complete asset
  set; probe-result shape/order and required instruction/skill safety coverage were incomplete.
- The old fixture worker ID did not match the frozen H00 `normandy` identity.
